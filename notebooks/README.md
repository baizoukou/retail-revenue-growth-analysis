# Jupyter Notebooks

This folder is where I'll keep the Jupyter notebooks for my Online Retail II analysis.

I'll be using Python, Pandas, and SQL to explore the data, understand sales patterns, and find out what's driving changes in business performance.

I've divided the work into four notebooks so I can focus on one stage at a time. I'll explain what I'm doing, why I'm doing it, and what I learn along the way.

## Planned Notebooks

### 01 — Data Quality Assessment
`01_data_quality.ipynb`

Before getting into the analysis, I want to make sure I understand the data I'm working with.

I'll check for missing values, duplicates, unusual prices, negative quantities, and cancelled orders. I'll also investigate whether these are actual errors or normal business transactions that need to be handled differently.

The goal is to identify potential problems and decide how to deal with them without losing useful information.

### 02 — Exploratory Data Analysis
`02_exploratory_analysis.ipynb`

Once I've assessed the data quality, I'll start exploring sales performance.

I'll look at how sales changed over time, which products sold the most, where customers are buying from, and how purchasing behaviour differs across the dataset.

I'll use charts to make the patterns easier to spot and identify anything that deserves a closer look.

### 03 — Growth Driver Analysis
`03_growth_driver_analysis.ipynb`

This is where I'll try to understand what's behind the changes in sales.

For example, if revenue increased during a particular month, was it because more customers placed orders, existing customers bought more, or certain products became more popular?

I'll compare countries, products, and customer groups to understand which segments contributed most to the changes.

### 04 — Business Insights and Recommendations
`04_business_recommendations.ipynb`

In the final notebook, I'll bring together what I've found and consider what it means for the business.

I'll focus on recommendations that make sense based on the evidence, particularly around stock planning, customer retention, and sales performance.

I'll also be clear about what the data cannot tell me, so I don't draw conclusions that the analysis doesn't support.

## How I'll Document My Analysis

For each notebook, I'll try to answer five questions:

1. What am I trying to find out?
2. Why does it matter to the business?
3. How am I analysing the data?
4. What do the results show?
5. What can I reasonably conclude from the findings?

I'll include the code, charts, explanations, and any issues I come across.

I want someone looking at my GitHub project to be able to follow my thinking, understand how I reached my conclusions, and reproduce the analysis.
