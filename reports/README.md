# Business Reports and Findings

This folder is where I'll keep the reports from my Online Retail II project.

The Jupyter notebooks will show how I analysed the data, while these reports will focus on what I found and what the results could mean for the business.

I want to go beyond presenting numbers and charts. My aim is to understand what's happening in the business, explain the reasons behind changes in performance, and identify areas where the findings could support better decisions.

## Planned Reports

### 01 — Data Quality Report
`01_data_quality_report.md`

I'll start by looking at how reliable the data is before using it to assess business performance.

This report will cover issues such as missing customer information, duplicate records, unusual transaction values, returns, and cancellations.

I'll explain what I found, how I dealt with the issues, and whether any of them could affect the results.

If I remove or exclude records during cleaning, I'll document why and check how this changes the figures.

### 02 — Sales Performance Report
`02_sales_performance_report.md`

This report will look at how sales changed over time.

I'll compare monthly revenue, order volumes, and average order values to understand when the business performed well and when sales slowed down.

I'll also look for seasonal patterns and investigate any noticeable changes.

Rather than simply reporting that sales went up or down, I want to understand what might have contributed to those movements.

### 03 — Revenue Growth Analysis
`03_revenue_growth_report.md`

Here, I'll focus on what actually drives revenue growth.

For example, if revenue increases in a particular month, I'll investigate whether it came from more orders, higher spending per order, or stronger sales from certain products or countries.

I'll also look at which parts of the business contributed most to growth and which experienced a decline.

The aim is to explain the changes using evidence from the data.

### 04 — Customer and Product Insights
`04_customer_product_insights.md`

This report will bring together what I've learned about customers and products.

I'll look at which products generate the most revenue, how frequently customers make purchases, and whether sales depend heavily on a small number of customers or products.

I'll also consider the impact of returns and missing customer information.

I'm interested in whether these findings could help the business improve stock planning, understand customer behaviour, or identify opportunities to increase repeat purchases.

### 05 — Business Recommendations
`05_business_recommendations.md`

This will be the final report, where I'll bring together the most important findings from the project.

I'll focus on a few recommendations that the business could realistically consider.

Depending on what the analysis shows, these could relate to inventory planning, seasonal demand, customer retention, or improving sales performance.

For each recommendation, I'll explain what I found, why it matters, and what action could be worth considering.

I'll also point out where the evidence is limited or where more information would be needed before making a decision.

## How I'll Write My Reports

I want the reports to be easy to read, even for someone who isn't familiar with Python or SQL.

I'll keep them focused on a few important questions:

- What business problem am I looking at?
- What did I find in the data?
- What evidence supports the findings?
- Why does it matter to the business?
- What could the business do with this information?
- Are there any limitations I need to consider?

I'll use charts and calculations to support the findings and make sure the numbers agree with my Python and SQL analysis.

## Tools and Format

I'll write the reports in **Markdown** so they can be viewed directly on GitHub.

Where useful, I'll include charts from the `figures/` folder and links to the relevant notebooks or SQL queries.

This way, someone interested in the business findings can read the reports, while anyone who wants to understand the technical work can explore the analysis behind them.
